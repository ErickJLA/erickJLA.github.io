Gas = (P − water vapour) × T_ref / T: temperature and humidity removed, what is left is gas added or removed. CO₂ partial pressure = ppm × P × T_ref / T, same units, so the two can be compared and subtracted: gas − CO₂ = the O₂ (and anything else) that changed.

Forced recalibration (fresh outdoor air, chamber open)

Put the SCD30 in outdoor air (or at an open window, nobody breathing nearby) for 2–5 min until it is stable.

Automatic self-calibration

Must stay off: in a scrubbed chamber it would learn ~0 ppm as “fresh air”.

SCD30 measurement interval

Longer = less heat in the chamber (19 mA at 2 s, ~6 mA at 15 s) but slower response.
