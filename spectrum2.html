The AS7341's own LED driver (LDR pin: the breakout's onboard LED, or your LED if it is wired there). 4–258 mA; stay within your LED's rating. Switched off when the app disconnects.
Counts scale with gain × integration time. Half the gain with twice the integration gives the same counts but collects twice the light per frame: less shot noise, bubbles and LED flicker averaged out, slower frames. Use the Noise view with the same sample in the clamp to compare settings.

How it works and which controls prove it
With medium only, the red channels see LED leak, a fixed fraction k of the excitation channel. The reference learns k; with cells the app predicts the leak as k × excitation now and shows what is left: the candidate fluorescence. Index = excess ÷ excitation in medium (independent of gain and integration).

Procedure: manual gain and integration with the excitation channel below ~90% of full scale in medium → Dark at those settings → medium, Set leak reference → culture: read the verdict (detected = >5σ and ≥10% above leak).
Controls: ink or black dye matched in absorbance and diluted milk (scatter) should stay near 1.00× leak; 850 nm excitation should give no excess; dark-adapted culture should show induction over the first tens of seconds; a dilution series should rise and flatten.
